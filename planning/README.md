# planning/

Your plans for the books: outlines, beat sheets, character notes for things not written yet, ideas. **Nothing here is a source.**

- **Never parsed.** Nothing in this folder goes into `data/` or the app. It isn't cited, it isn't checked for contradictions, and a reparse ignores changes here.
- **Readable when discussing the story.** The `query` skill may read these files while you plan or write, and always labels what it takes from them as *plan*, separate from what the written books say (*canon*).
- **The books win where they exist.** Where the written chapters and the plan disagree, the chapters are what's in the DB. Claude points the difference out and leaves the choice to you.
- **Yours to edit.** AI sessions don't change these files unless you ask for that specific edit.
